The axi0 suite on your phone without a browser around it: Notes, Drive, Photos, Mail, Meet, Calendar, Tasks, Docs
and the rest of the suite's applets, each in its own task with its own name and icon in Recents.

* The icon opens the suite's launcher; long-press it for Lock, the applet list and the applets you opened last.
* Links to search.axi0.io from any app open here, in their applet.
* Your axi0 Key stays inside the app: tick "Stay on this browser" and it survives restarts; it never goes to a
  browser or a backup. Lock wipes it from the phone; your lockers stay on the Keep.
* A Key you make is saved to Downloads as a .axi0key file; files you save from the pages go to Downloads too.
* Meet and Recorder ask for the camera and microphone when they need them; location is off unless you switch it on.
* Reminders from Tasks and Calendar as notifications, sent ahead while your Key is open there. They say what kind
  and how many ("2 tasks due"), not what: titles only if you turn on Show titles in the app's Notifications settings.
* Share to axi0 from any app: text becomes a note (axi0 Notes), pictures and videos go up to axi0 Photos, any other
  file to axi0 Drive, once your Key is open.
* Everything that is not search.axi0.io opens in your browser.

Built on Capacitor and its Local Notifications plugin (MIT).
