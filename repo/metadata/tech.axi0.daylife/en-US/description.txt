0daylife is the Flux0S home screen for Android 12 and newer, made for GrapheneOS phones.

The home opens on the depth clock, the day over the time in the house font, set into your wallpaper. Under it, pages of a grid you size yourself, a glass dock, your apps, and the axi0 suite's tiles (Search, Mail, Calendar, Notes, Drive, Photos, Tasks, Kan0, 0Games, 0Auth and the rest), each opening its page in your browser.

- Swipe up for the drawer: search your apps by name or alias ("spreadsheet" finds Sheets); the last row searches the web at search.axi0.io.
- Glass panels: the wallpaper and the home blur behind every panel; flat panels where blur is off.
- Widgets from a gallery, with an edit mode: drag, sizes in whole cells, remove.
- Folders by dropping one icon on another; long-press menus on icons, folders, widgets and the wallpaper.
- Quick settings show our apps' states only, from Axi0s Services: Key unlocked, last sync, "3 new" per app, never what is new.
- Settings: make it your home app, the grid, blur, the clock, labels, and Forget everything.
- No analytics, no crash reporting, no account, no INTERNET permission. The only thing that leaves is the search you hand to your browser.
